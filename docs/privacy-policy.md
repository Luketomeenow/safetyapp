# Axxiom Safety Assistant: privacy notice

Draft for Axxiom to review, adjust and publish at a public URL. Apple requires that URL before the app can be submitted. Written for the technicians who use the app, so it is deliberately plain.

_Last updated: [date]_

## What this app is

The Axxiom Safety Assistant is an internal tool for Axxiom Elevator employees. It answers questions about Axxiom's Safety and Health Policies and shows the policy text behind every answer. Axxiom provides it; it is not available to the public.

## What we collect

- **Your account.** Your work email address and a user identifier, issued by Axxiom. You cannot sign up yourself.
- **Your questions and the answers you receive**, including which policy pages were cited and whether you marked an answer helpful or reported it.
- **Basic technical information** about each request: the app version, timing, and whether the answer succeeded. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.
- **Dictation.** If you use the microphone to ask a question, speech is converted to text on your iPhone where the device supports it. The audio is not sent to Axxiom or stored.

## Why we collect it

To answer your questions, and to let Axxiom's Safety Manager review the assistant's answers for accuracy. Questions and answers are treated as safety records. They may be reviewed after an incident, in the same way other safety documentation is.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell it.

## Who else sees it

- **Anthropic** processes the text of your question, together with the safety manual, to generate the answer. Anthropic does not use this content to train its models.
- **Service providers** that host the app's database, files and monitoring on Axxiom's behalf, under contract.

Inside Axxiom, the Safety Manager and the team that maintains the app can see questions and answers. Your supervisor is not sent your questions.

## How long we keep it

Questions and answers are kept for [three years] as safety records, then deleted. Your account is removed when you leave the company or when Axxiom disables it.

## Your choices

- You can ask what data is held about you, and ask for your account and chat history to be deleted. Use "Request my data or account deletion" in the app's Settings, or email [safety@axxiomelevator.com]. Requests are handled within 30 days.
- Deleting the app removes the copy of the manual and the conversation history stored on your iPhone. It does not delete the records held by Axxiom.
- Dictation is optional. You can always type instead.

## A note about the assistant itself

The assistant can be wrong. The written policy and your supervisor are the authority, and the app is not a substitute for training, a Job Hazard Analysis or a permit. In an emergency, call 911.
