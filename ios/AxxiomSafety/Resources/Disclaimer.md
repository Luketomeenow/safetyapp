# Before you use the Safety Assistant

The assistant answers questions using only the Axxiom Elevator Safety and Health Policies manual and shows you the exact policy text it relied on.

- The written policy and your supervisor are the authority. If the assistant and a supervisor disagree, follow your supervisor and report the difference to the Safety Manager.
- It is not a substitute for training, a Job Hazard Analysis, or a permit.
- In an emergency, call 911 first. The Emergency button is on every screen.
- Every question and answer is logged so the safety team can review and improve the assistant.

Tap "I understand" to continue.
